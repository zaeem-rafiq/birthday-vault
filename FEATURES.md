# Features

What Birthday Vault does today, in plain words, and the test that proves each feature. This is the product's contract: change a row in the same pull request as the feature, and `./check` fails when a proof is missing. Planned work belongs in PLAN.md, not here.

Proof names a test file and, after `#`, the name of the test inside it. The first proof is the feature's end-to-end test, which runs the real contracts on a local NEAR sandbox. Several proofs are separated by commas.

| Feature | What it does | How to reach it | Proof | Broken looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Try an example creates a play vault | A visitor types a nickname and gets a vault on NEAR's test network, with no login, play money and a short lock. The vault account has no keys | Open the site, type a nickname, press **Try an example** | `web/e2e/try-example.spec.ts#visitor creates a vault with no login`, `web/test/demo.test.ts#accepts nicknames of letters, digits and spaces up to 24` | The button spins forever, shows an error, or the vault page shows a key count other than 0 |
| A play gift grows the tree | A $1 play gift raises the vault balance, and the tree grows from a seed to a sprout | On a new example vault, press **Send a $1 play gift** | `web/e2e/gift.spec.ts#play gift raises balance and grows the tree`, `web/test/tree.test.ts#never shrinks when the balance grows` | The balance stays at $0.00, or the tree does not change |
| Early withdrawal is refused | Before the lock ends, the contract refuses a withdrawal and the money stays | On an example vault with a gift, press **Try to withdraw early** | `web/e2e/lock.spec.ts#early withdrawal is refused` | The money leaves the vault, or no refusal is shown |
| Transfer after unlock moves the money rights | After the lock, the NFT goes to a new owner. The old owner is refused and the new owner withdraws | Wait for the lock to end, press **Transfer it**, then the two withdraw buttons | `web/e2e/transfer.spec.ts#new owner withdraws and old owner is refused` | The old owner can withdraw, or the new owner cannot |
| Child can claim after unlock | If the guardian loses their key, the child account named at mint claims the vault and withdraws | Wait for the lock to end, press **The guardian lost their key**, then the child's withdraw button | `web/e2e/claim.spec.ts#child claims when guardian key is lost` | The claim is refused, or the money does not move |
| Gift page shows a live quote and never sends a testnet id | The gift page asks NEAR Intents for a no-money price from Base, Arbitrum, Ethereum or Solana, labelled "Preview only. Nothing is sent." The quote never names a testnet account | On a vault page, follow the gift link and enter a refund address | `web/e2e/quote.spec.ts#gift page shows a preview quote`, `web/e2e/quote.spec.ts#gift page explains a slow quote service and can retry`, `web/test/quote.test.ts#says the service is slow on a server error or a timeout`, `web/test/quote.test.ts#never sends a testnet or sandbox id to 1Click` | No price appears, the page waits forever when NEAR Intents is slow, the preview label is missing, or a testnet id reaches 1Click |
| Real gift control is hidden on the public build | The site never asks for a deposit address, so nobody can send real money through it | Open any gift page | `web/e2e/quote.spec.ts#deposit address control is hidden`, `web/test/quote.test.ts#only ever asks for dry quotes` | A deposit address or a "Get deposit address" button appears |
| Unknown vault ids are refused | A vault address that the collection does not know shows "Vault not found" and no actions | Open `/vault/?id=` with any other address | `web/e2e/lock.spec.ts#unknown vault id is refused`, `web/test/network.test.ts#accepts only bv-<n> vaults of the configured collection` | A made-up address shows a vault, a balance or buttons |
| Limits are shown | A plain page lists what the product does not do, linked from every page's footer | Press **Limits** at the bottom of any page | `web/e2e/limits.spec.ts#limits page lists the known limits` | The page is missing, or a limit is missing |
| Network errors are explained | When the test network does not answer, the vault page says so, keeps the vault safe, and offers Try again instead of loading forever | Open a vault page while the network is down | `web/e2e/network.spec.ts#vault page explains an unreachable network and recovers`, `web/test/demo.test.ts#unwraps a failover error to the provider's own message` | The page shows a blank, a spinner forever, or a wrong $0.00 balance |
